How to play

  1. Skill shot. Plunge so the ball drops through the flashing lane at the top.
  2. S, E, A lanes. Roll through all three to raise the end-of-ball bonus, up to 5×. The flippers move the lit lanes.
  3. Lock and multiball. Knock down the three red targets to light Lock, then shoot the lamp room, the cup on the upper left. Three locks start multiball, where every ramp and orbit is a jackpot.
  4. Watches. With no lock lit, the lamp room starts a 30-second watch: Fog (40 spinner turns), Squall (30 bumper hits) or Rescue (follow the lit shot). Finish two for an extra ball and all three for Long Night.
  5. Kickback. The left outlane kicks the ball back once. Three ramps relight it.
  6. Nudge. A nudge can save a ball. Three in quick succession and the table warns you; the third warning tilts it.

Controls

  • Touch Left and right halves of the screen work the flippers. With the ball on the plunger, hold the right half and let go to launch. Flick up to nudge.
  • Keys Z and / (or the Shift keys) flip. Hold Space to pull the plunger. Up arrow, X and full stop nudge. Esc pauses.
  • Gamepad Bumpers or triggers flip, A launches, Y nudges.
